## Summary 
Ocean's 8 is a slick crime comedy centered on a high-stakes jewelry heist at New York City's star-studded Met Gala.
The plot follows a master thief who, immediately after being released from prison, puts a meticulous plan into motion to steal a $150 million diamond necklace. Instead of using force, the operation relies on a specialized team of experts who use hacking, sleight of hand, high fashion, and clever disguises to execute the theft in plain sight. They successfully swap the target jewels with replicas, sneak the real diamonds out of the museum, and frame a deceitful adversary for the crime, ultimately walking away with a massive fortune.
